EGYPTIAN YOUTH AI CHALLENGE

Learn. Think. Challenge Yourself.

The Egyptian Youth AI Challenge (EYAC) is an Artificial Intelligence competition designed for students who are interested in AI and want to test their knowledge, problem-solving skills, and understanding of fundamental AI concepts.

EYAC brings together school students from Grades 9–12 and university students in a competitive learning environment where participants can challenge themselves and demonstrate what they know about Artificial Intelligence.

Competition Focus

EYAC focuses on key areas of Artificial Intelligence and encourages participants to develop both their understanding and problem-solving abilities.

AI Fundamentals

Understanding the core concepts and principles behind AI.

Machine Learning

Exploring how machines learn from data and identify patterns.

AI Applications

Understanding how AI can be applied to different fields and problems.

Problem Solving

Applying knowledge and logical thinking to AI-related challenges.

The challenge is designed to welcome students with an interest in Artificial Intelligence and provide them with an opportunity to test their knowledge in a competitive setting.

Competition Format

EYAC combines AI-focused questions and challenges designed to test participants' understanding. Participants are challenged to:

  • Apply their knowledge of AI concepts
  • Analyze AI-related problems
  • Use logical reasoning
  • Demonstrate their understanding of fundamental AI topics
  • Challenge themselves in a competitive environment

What Participants Gain

  • Test Their Knowledge: Measure their understanding of AI concepts.
  • Challenge Their Thinking: Work through questions that require reasoning and careful analysis.
  • Explore AI: Discover more about the concepts behind modern AI.
  • Compete & Learn: Experience AI learning in a competitive environment alongside other students.

Beyond the Challenge

EYAC is part of our effort to encourage young people to explore Artificial Intelligence and develop a stronger understanding of the field. We believe that competitions can give students a reason to learn beyond their regular curriculum, test what they know, and discover areas they may want to explore further.
