Tecto-Power Project

Predicting earthquakes and harnessing their immense energy to generate electricity.

Project Overview

TectoPower is a comprehensive solution to the global earthquake crisis.

Earthquakes are a powerful natural force that can release enormous amounts of energy, strike without warning, destroy cities, and cause significant loss of life.

Our project aims to address three major challenges associated with earthquakes: prediction, energy utilization, and structural protection.

First, we developed an AI-powered earthquake prediction model trained on data collected from thousands of sensors that have recorded seismic activity from previous earthquakes. We also incorporated additional factors, including the potential relationship between earthquake activity and lunar movements, as well as low-frequency seismic waves and other signals that may occur before an earthquake. By analyzing these patterns, the model can estimate the probability of future earthquake activity at different levels of likelihood.

Second, we developed a prototype capable of generating electrical energy from earthquake vibrations, based on Faraday’s law of electromagnetic induction. The concept aims to transform part of the mechanical energy produced by seismic movement into usable electrical energy.

Finally, to reduce the destructive impact of earthquakes on buildings, we drew inspiration from Japanese earthquake-resistant engineering techniques. Our design incorporates spring-based isolation systems beneath buildings, allowing the structure to absorb and reduce seismic vibrations, helping protect buildings from severe structural damage or collapse.

Through these three integrated solutions, TectoPower aims to transform earthquakes from an unpredictable and destructive threat into a phenomenon that can be better predicted, partially utilized as an energy source, and mitigated through innovative structural protection.

Core Innovations

  • AI Model: It works to predict earthquakes more than six hours before they occur.
  • Electricity from Earthquakes: The ability to convert a large amount of earthquake energy into electrical energy.
