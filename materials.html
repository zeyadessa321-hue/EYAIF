Where to Go Next

Artificial Intelligence is a broad field, and learners can follow different paths depending on their interests.

Those interested in Machine Learning can continue with structured courses and practical tools.

Those interested in Deep Learning can move toward neural networks and frameworks such as PyTorch and TensorFlow.

Those interested in modern AI can explore Large Language Models, Generative AI, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, and AI Agents.

Learners interested in research can progress toward university-level courses, research papers, open-source implementations, and current research communities.


EYAIF will continue expanding this collection with additional educational materials, projects, and resources as the foundation develops.
